Methodology

Starts count alerts beginning in the calendar month. Time under alert is the non-overlapping union of intervals within Europe/Kyiv calendar days. Mean full duration belongs to the month in which each alert started, even if it ends later. Daily display values are rounded, while monthly aggregates use raw seconds.
